## Findings (all reproduced)

**1. High, high confidence (C8, V3, AC 1 and 11). The skill silently drops cues and merges cues on malformed input, which PROOF.md and SKILL.md say cannot happen.**
- Input `WEBVTT\n00:00:01.000 --> 00:00:02.000\n<v Ada Example>First words.</v>\n\n00:00:03.000 --> …<v Ben Example>Second.</v>` returns status `written`, 1 utterance (Ben's). Ada's cue disappears with no warning. Cause: `blocks[1:]` throws away the whole header block.
- The same happens with `WEBVTT Kind: captions\nLanguage: en\n<cue>`.
- Two unlabeled cues with no blank line between them become one utterance whose text is `'First words.\n00:00:03.000 --> 00:00:04.000\nSecond.'`.
- This contradicts "Cues are never merged or split", "Each WebVTT cue becomes one utterance" and SKILL §9's "If any part fails, it reports UNPARSEABLE". It also breaks AC 11 (a partial record that looks complete). It does not affect the committed sample record.
- Fix: raise Unparseable when the header block contains `-->`, or when any body line contains `-->`.

**2. Medium, high confidence (C9, C1). The test suite misses 4 mutants.**
- Removing all Markdown escaping (`escape_md` returns `text`) keeps all 22 green. markdown-it then renders `> *stars*` as `<em>stars</em>` and `1. not a list` as an `<ol>`. So `verbatim_text_survives_markdown_rendering` never renders anything.
- Also survived: no title redaction in the record, a provenance `skill_sha256` of `0`, and a partial escape removal.
- Killed (16): phone redaction off, overwrite allowed, merging same-speaker cues, unlabeled cues given to the previous speaker, writing before parsing finishes, git-ignore check skipped, participant/ check skipped, no rollback, no temp cleanup, entity decoding, instruction flag, label redaction, provenance redaction, order check, unknown label marked certain, ingested_at omitted.
- Fix: assert the rendered HTML text equals the input, and assert title redaction and skill_sha256.

**3. High, high confidence (C8, V3, process). PROOF.md states things Beau has not yet done.**
- "I read the work and ran the tests myself before submitting it" was drafted by the AI, in the first person, before any submission. The attempt is still `in_progress`.
- The "Independent review … It approved" paragraph points to no artifact a reviewer can check.
- Fix: Beau confirms or rewrites the first sentence himself, and the review claim either links its record or is softened.

**4. Medium, high confidence (C8). Redaction misses forms the limitations list leaves out.**
- `317-555-0198x12` and `317-555-0143ext` are left in clear. Cause: `(?!\w)` fails when letters follow the number.
- So are `317 555 0148` and `317&nbsp;555&nbsp;0149`.
- SKILL §8 says the `nnn nnn nnnn` form is redacted, and neither the limitations list nor SKILL.md mentions extensions or non-breaking spaces.
- Fix: allow an `x`/`ext` tail and ` ` separators, or list both as limitations.

**5. Low (C9 tooling).** `INGEST_UNDER_TEST` only works if the copy sits at `<root>/participant/skills/x/ingest.py`. Anywhere else, `REPO = parents[2]` falls back to the git check and all 22 tests fail. I worked around it by placing each mutant in that layout. Fix: say so in the docstring.
